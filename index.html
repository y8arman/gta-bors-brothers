    Controls

    Mouse (default on desktop): hold the left button to walk toward the cursor, further away means run. Right button or Space shoots. Click a car to walk over and get in. In a car: hold the left button to drive toward the cursor, right button brakes and reverses, Space drifts, E gets out.

    Keyboard: WASD moves, Shift walks, E enters and exits cars, 1 to 9 or the mouse wheel switch weapons (5 taser, 6 bazooka, 7 grenade, 8 molotov, 9 donut decoy; mouse wheel or Q for sniper, katana and C4), F signature move, G drive-by, X detonates C4, N nitro (after the garage upgrade), J opens the mission list, Esc opens the menu.

    Phone: left thumb moves or drives. Right thumb: hold to shoot with auto-aim, drag to aim yourself. Tap a car to get in.
